# PixelForge Design System: Master Specifications

## 1. Brand Identity & Vision
- **Product**: PixelForge
- **Category**: AI Design & Creative Production Platform
- **Tagline**: “Turn ideas into production-ready designs.”
- **Mood**: Creative editorial aesthetic meets futuristic creative technology. High contrast, sophisticated, cinematic, spacious, with selective neon luminescence.

## 2. Color System
- **Background Deep Black**: `#050508`
- **Surface Deep**: `#08080C`
- **Card Neutral**: `#0D0D14`
- **Card Highlight**: `#12121D`
- **Border Default**: `#232336`
- **Border Hover**: `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`
- **Off-White Text Primary**: `#F5F5F7`
- **Text Muted**: `#8E8EA8`
- **Text Subtle**: `#52526B`
- **Neon Accents**:
  - Electric Violet: `#8B5CF6` / `#7C3AED`
  - Neon Cyan: `#06B6D4` / `#22D3EE`
  - Hot Pink: `#F43F5E` / `#EC4899`
  - Status Amber: `#F59E0B`

## 3. Typography
- **Headings / Display**: *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Weight 600, 700, 800, 900), tight letter spacing (`tracking-tight`), editorial hierarchy.
- **Body**: *Inter* (Weight 400, 500, 600), clean, high readability.
- **Controls & Metadata**: *JetBrains Mono* (Weight 400, 500), uppercase tracking for technical tags and layer properties.

## 4. Components Architecture
- `Navbar`: Minimal, sticky, backdrop blur, logo, nav links, Sign In, Primary CTA "Start Creating".
- `Hero`: Editorial headline, neon gradient accents, floating creative workspace mockup with artboards, AI prompt bar, color tokens, and layer system.
- `LogoCloud`: Minimalist monochrome wordmarks (NOVA, ARC, FRAME, KINETIC, MONO, ORBIT).
- `ProblemSolution`: Editorial split section highlighting creative friction vs. unified production.
- `FeatureGrid`: 5 distinct feature compositions with custom interactive visual cards.
- `CreativeShowcase`: Magazine-style asymmetrical collage of fictional projects (ORBIT, NEXUS, VOID, AURA, MONO, PULSE).
- `Workflow`: 4-step horizontal process (01 IDEA → 02 EXPLORE → 03 BUILD → 04 SHIP).
- `CanvasShowcase`: Immersive design canvas mockup with multi-artboard controls and live token inspectors.
- `AIAssistant`: Interactive futuristic copilot conversation with 4 generated directions and refinement actions.
- `BeforeAfter`: Interactive before/after comparison slider between rough idea and production-ready visual system.
- `UseCases`: 8 dedicated creative team profiles (Brand Design, Campaigns, Product UI, etc.).
- `Testimonials`: 3 authentic creative director & studio founder perspectives.
- `Pricing`: Starter ($0), Creative ($19/mo, highlighted), Studio ($49/mo).
- `FAQ`: 7 expandable accordion questions.
- `FinalCTA`: Full-width climax with abstract neon gradient shape and interactive celebration.
- `Footer`: Categorized navigation, copyright, and platform status.
- `Modal`: Universal multi-mode creative workspace modal (Free Trial, Demo, Sign-In, Project Export).
